# Photosynthesis

## What is photosynthesis?
Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make their own food using sunlight, carbon dioxide and water. It happens mainly in the leaves.

## Where it happens
Chloroplasts are the parts of a plant cell where photosynthesis takes place. They contain chlorophyll, the green pigment that absorbs sunlight.

## Raw materials
- Carbon dioxide enters through tiny pores in the leaf called stomata.
- Water is absorbed by the roots and carried to the leaves through the xylem.
- Sunlight is absorbed by chlorophyll.

## The equation
Carbon dioxide + water, in the presence of sunlight and chlorophyll, gives glucose + oxygen.

## Products
Glucose is used for energy or stored as starch. Oxygen is released into the air through the stomata.

## Why it matters
Photosynthesis supplies the oxygen we breathe and forms the base of almost every food chain.
